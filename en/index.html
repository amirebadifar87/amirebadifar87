Mehriz Elderly Care Home Website

The official, live website of Mehriz charitable elderly care home, bringing together the centre's story, news, admissions and paths for donors and volunteers.

The problem
A charitable care centre needs to explain its services and admissions clearly, publish news and articles, and make donations, in-kind giving and volunteering accessible to different audiences.
My responsibility
Website development and maintenance, turning the centre's real needs into usable sections and workflows in a live product.

Key capabilities

  • Centre, clinic and day-care rehabilitation services
  • News and articles with dedicated pages
  • Admission requirements and application steps
  • Donation information and payment details
  • Production website
  • Content management
  • Interactive forms
  • Participation workflows

The source is private, so its internal stack is not claimed here. The items above are capabilities visible in the published release.
